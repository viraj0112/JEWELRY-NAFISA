Dagina Role Handbook

Everything each of the four account types on Dagina can do, and the exact route to doing it. Your role is set on your account record and decides which app you land in after sign-in — members browse and request quotes, designers and manufacturers run their own catalogue, and admins operate the platform behind all three.

Member

Copper · Consumer app

The person shopping for a piece. You browse the catalogue, collect what you like onto boards, spend credits to unlock a piece's full metal-and-stone specification, and send a quote request to have it made.

How you get this role
Any ordinary sign-up — email, phone or Google. It is the default.
Where you land
Home feed, with Boards, Search, Notifications and Info along the bottom.
Your currency
Credits, refreshed monthly. Members get a larger monthly allowance than non-members.
Approval needed
None. You can browse immediately.

Getting set up

Create your account

Welcome screen → Continue with Email / Continue with Phone / Google

  1. Pick a sign-in method. Email needs a password and a confirmation click in your inbox; phone sends you a one-time code.
  2. Confirm your address or code. Until you do, your account cannot sign in.
  3. You land on the home feed straight away — no waiting for review.

If it goes wrongSign-up says the address is already registered: that account exists but may never have been confirmed. Use Log in and request a fresh confirmation mail rather than signing up again.

Finish your taste profile

Appears on its own about 45 seconds after you first reach the home feed

Five short steps that tune your feed: location and PIN code, gender, age, the occasions you shop for, and your jewellery categories.

  1. Answer each screen — you can change every answer later from your profile.
  2. Your progress is saved after each step, so leaving halfway resumes where you stopped.

If it goes wrongIt never appeared: it only runs for members who have not finished setup, and it waits 45 seconds on purpose so you can look around first.

Invite friends for credits

Profile → Referrals

  1. Copy your referral code from the referral card.
  2. Share it. When someone signs up with it, both of you are awarded credits.
  3. Your referral history lists who joined and how many credits each invite earned.

Finding pieces

Filter the feed by metal

Home → the metal row above the grid

The row switches the whole feed between metal types. Get it is the ready-to-buy catalogue — pieces you can order immediately rather than have made.

  1. Tap a metal to switch the feed.
  2. With Get it selected, a second row appears for the specific ready-stock ranges.
  3. Tap the same metal again, or use Reset, to clear it.

Narrow by specification

Home → Filters panel

Filter on the attributes a jeweller actually cares about: jewellery type, metal weight range, metal colour and purity, enamel work, stone weight, shape, type, quality and setting, plus featured tags.

  1. Open the filter panel and set the attributes you want.
  2. Apply. The grid reloads against your selection.
  3. Reset clears everything back to the open catalogue.

If it goes wrongAn empty grid usually means the combination is too narrow — clear the stone filters first, since those apply only to studded pieces.

Search by words or by photo

Search tab

  1. Type a design, category or style into the search bar for text results.
  2. Or tap Search by Image and choose Camera or Gallery to find visually similar pieces.
  3. Recent searches are kept so you can jump back to one.

If it goes wrongImage search fails on very dark, blurred or heavily cropped photos. Re-shoot the piece square-on against a plain background.

Collecting and sharing

Save to a board

Any product card or detail page → bookmark icon

  1. Tap the bookmark on a piece.
  2. Choose an existing board, or create one on the spot.
  3. Mark a board secret while creating it to keep it private.

Manage your boards

Boards tab

  • Open a board to see everything saved in it.
  • Rename a board or switch its secret setting at any time.
  • Deleting a board removes the board, not the pieces from the catalogue.

Like and share

Product card, or the detail page header

Likes are counted live and feed the demand signals sellers see. Share sends a direct link to the piece that opens without the recipient needing an account.

Quotes and credits

Reveal a piece's full specification

Product detail → Get details

Full metal purity, weights, stone details and dimensions are hidden until you unlock them. Unlocking costs credits — the exact number is set by the platform, commonly 5 per piece.

  1. Open the piece and tap Get details.
  2. Confirm the credit spend. The specification table and any additional images unlock immediately.
  3. Once unlocked, that piece stays unlocked for you.

If it goes wrongAt 80% of your allowance spent you get a warning dialog. At zero, unlocking is blocked until your monthly refresh — or until you upgrade your membership.

Request a quote

Product detail → Get Quote

  1. Tap Get Quote on an unlocked piece.
  2. Adjust the specification you want quoted — metal type, purity, colour, finish and weight, and for studded pieces the stone type, cut, colour, count, purity, weight and setting.
  3. Add a phone number if your profile has none, plus any notes.
  4. Submit. It reaches the quote desk and you are contacted with pricing.

If it goes wrongSubmission fails on a piece you have not unlocked — the quote carries the full specification, so reveal the details first.

Track your quotes

Profile → View Detail History

Shows every quote you have raised, with totals for how many are still valid and how many have expired.

Upgrade your membership

Profile → Membership

Membership raises your monthly credit allowance, so you can unlock more pieces between refreshes.

  1. Open the membership card in your profile.
  2. Choose a plan and complete checkout.
  3. Your new allowance applies from the next credit refresh.

Designer

Patina · Business app

A 3D designer, sketch artist or independent studio selling designs through Dagina. You run your own catalogue: upload pieces, watch what members engage with, and answer the demand you see. Everything you upload passes moderation before it goes live.

How you get this role
Business sign-up with type 3D Designer, Sketch Artist or Other.
Approval needed
Yes — documents reviewed within 2–3 business days.
Where you land
Business app: Home, Upload, Insights, Notifications, Profile.
Plan gating
Geographic demand analytics require Premium.

Joining and approval

Apply for a business account

Sign-up → “Are you a designer?”

  1. Enter your email and password.
  2. Add your business or full name, business type, phone, address and GST number if you have one.
  3. Upload two documents: a portfolio or work file showing samples of your work, and your business card.
  4. Submit, then confirm your email address. Your application goes to review.

If it goes wrongBoth documents are required — the form will not submit with one missing. If the address is already registered you are told before anything uploads.

While you wait for approval

Pending approval screen, shown at sign-in

Your account exists and your documents are filed, but the catalogue tools stay locked until an admin approves you. Reviews take 2–3 business days.

If it goes wrongStill pending after three working days: ask support to check Moderation → Verification Requests. Documents uploaded before you confirmed your email sit in a separate holding area and are still visible to reviewers.

Building your catalogue

Upload one piece

Upload → Single product

A guided wizard covering the full specification sheet.

  1. Add images, then the basics: title, description, price, SKU, tags and sub-category.
  2. Set the metal: type, purity (the options change with the metal), weight, colour, finish and plating.
  3. Choose product type and gender. Product type suggestions come from existing values — you can type a new one.
  4. Set jewellery type. Choosing Studded opens the stone fields: type, used, setting, cut, count, colour, weight, purity and quality.
  5. Add dimensions, enamel work and weight, and whether the piece is customisable.
  6. Review the summary and submit. The piece enters moderation.

If it goes wrongSwitching away from Studded clears the stone fields by design — set jewellery type before filling them in.

Upload in bulk

Upload → Bulk upload

  1. Download the sample CSV. Its headers match the catalogue exactly — keep them.
  2. Fill one row per piece. Every row needs either a Product Type or a Jewelry Type.
  3. Name image files after the row's SKU, numbering extra shots as -Image2, -Image3 and so on. Matching ignores case and orders numerically, so -Image2 stays ahead of -Image10.
  4. Pick the CSV, then select all the image files together.
  5. Submit. The result tells you how many rows went through and names any that were skipped, with the reason.

If it goes wrongA row whose images all failed to upload is skipped rather than published without pictures. Re-check those filenames against the SKU and upload just those rows again.

Edit your catalogue in a spreadsheet

Home → select products → Edit in Sheets

A full round trip: export, edit anywhere, sync back. SKU is the match key.

  1. Export — choose your columns and download. Select products on the grid first to export only those, or export everything.
  2. Edit in any spreadsheet. Multi-value columns use a | b | c.
  3. Import — pick the edited file. It is checked before anything is written and every problem row is listed with its sheet row number.
  4. Sync — rows whose SKU already exists overwrite that product. Unknown SKUs are added as new products only if you enable Publish new products.
  5. The History tab records each sync.

If it goes wrongOnly columns present in your file are written — deleting a column leaves that data untouched, but clearing a cell does clear the value.

Fill gaps with AI

Top bar → sparkle icon → AI Fill My Products

Completes empty attribute columns on your own products by reading their images. It never touches anyone else's catalogue.

  1. Optionally save your own provider key and preferred model — it is encrypted and never shown back to you. Leave the key blank to keep the one already saved, or remove it to fall back to the platform key.
  2. Choose how many rows to process.
  3. Run it. You get a filled-versus-total count, and the pieces it touched are flagged on your grid.

If it goes wrong“No API key assigned yet” means an admin has not issued you a key. Ask support — you cannot issue one yourself.

Find and remove pieces

Home → search bar, Filters, and the grid toolbar

  • Search across SKU, title, product type and category.
  • Filter by product type, category, metal type and demand level — the chips only ever show values that exist in your catalogue.
  • Switch between grid and list.
  • Select pieces and delete. Deletion removes the images and the engagement history too, and cannot be undone.

Watching demand

Read your insights

Insights tab

  • Headline counts for views, likes, shares and saves across your catalogue.
  • Top-performing products ranked by engagement.
  • Recent activity as it happens.

If it goes wrongCounts look flat on a new piece: engagement only starts accruing once moderation clears it and it appears in the member feed.

Per-piece insights

Home → any product card → insights

Opens that piece's own numbers, its demand level, and where in the country the interest is coming from.

Unlock geographic demand

Insights → the locked map card, or Profile → Upgrade

Region-level demand is a Premium feature for designers. Free accounts see the card with the map obscured.

  1. Open the upgrade banner from your profile or the locked card.
  2. Complete the upgrade. The map unlocks across Insights and the per-piece sheets.

Staying on top of it

Check what cleared moderation

Notifications → Approvals tab

Every piece you have submitted with its current state — pending, approved or rejected, newest first. The same badges appear on your product cards.

If it goes wrongApproved but not visible in the member catalogue: report it. Approval publishes the piece into the live catalogue, so an approved piece that never appears means the publish step failed and an admin needs to re-run it.

Activity and alerts

Notifications → Activity tab

Moderation outcomes, milestones and platform announcements. Mark all as read clears the badge.

Your business profile

Profile tab

  • Business name, type and contact details, with your account badge — Premium or Free.
  • Headline metrics for your catalogue.
  • Account settings, and sign out.

Manufacturer

Steel · Business app

A manufacturing house listing production-ready pieces. You use the same business app as designers, with the same upload, spreadsheet, AI-fill and insight tools — but your catalogue is kept separate, and demand analytics are open to you by default.

How you get this role
Business sign-up with type Manufacturer.
Approval needed
Yes — same 2–3 business day document review.
Your catalogue
Held separately from the designer catalogue. Filters and exports only ever cover your own pieces.
Plan gating
None. Geographic analytics are always unlocked; there is no premium tier.

What differs from a designer account

Everything in the Designer section applies

Switch the handbook to Designer for the full task-by-task detail

Applying, uploading singly or in bulk, Edit in Sheets, AI Fill, search and filters, deletion, insights, approvals and profile all work identically. The three differences below are the whole of it.

Geographic analytics are always on

Insights tab, and each product's insight sheet

Region-level demand is open to every manufacturer account. There is no locked card and no upgrade path, because manufacturers have no premium tier — the upgrade banner and premium badge simply do not appear for you.

A separate catalogue

Everywhere your products are listed

Manufacturer pieces live in their own catalogue, distinct from designer pieces. The app works this out from your profile, so your grid, filter chips, exports, AI fills and approval list are automatically scoped to your own stock.

If it goes wrongIf an account somehow holds both a designer and a manufacturer profile, the manufacturer one wins and your manufacturer catalogue is what loads. Flag it to support so the duplicate can be cleared.

Quotes reach you as demand, not as a desk

Insights & Notifications

Member quote requests are worked by the admin quote desk. What comes back to you is the signal: engagement counts, demand levels and the regions asking. Use them to decide what to list next.

Admin

Brass · Operations console

You run the platform. You decide which businesses get in, which pieces reach the catalogue, what a credit is worth, who holds an API key, and what every member and seller is told. Nine sections, reachable from the sidebar.

How you get this role
Granted directly on the account record. There is no self-service route.
Where you land
The admin console — Dashboard first.
Sections
Dashboard · Moderation · User Management · Quote Tracking · Teams · Analytics · Inventory · AI Data Fill · Settings
Blast radius
Credit and broadcast changes apply to every account at once. Read twice before saving.

Daily queue work

Clear the moderation queue

Moderation → Pending Submissions

Everything designers and manufacturers submit waits here. Approving publishes the piece into the live catalogue and notifies its owner; rejecting notifies them too.

  1. Review the submission — images, title, description, category and attributes.
  2. Approve or reject it. Select several and use the bulk action to clear a batch.
  3. Narrow the queue by source catalogue or by uploader email or business name when you are chasing a specific seller.
  4. Past decisions stay in the Archive tab.

If it goes wrongIf publishing the approved piece into the catalogue fails, you will see an error — do not treat the approval as done. The seller's Approvals tab will say approved while the piece is missing from the catalogue, which is exactly the report to escalate.

Vet business applications

Moderation → Verification Requests

  1. Open a pending applicant and read their portfolio or work file and their business card.
  2. Approve to release the business app to them, or reject.
  3. Approved sellers move straight from the pending screen into the business app on their next sign-in.

If it goes wrongNo documents listed: applicants who have not yet confirmed their email have their files in a pre-confirmation holding area. User Management pulls from there as well, so check the applicant's documents there before rejecting for “no documents”.

Work the quote desk

Quote Tracking

Every member quote request, with the full metal and stone specification they asked to be priced. Headline figures cover total quotes, active pending, response rate and closures this month.

  1. Filter by All, Pending, Responded or Closed.
  2. Open a quote to read the specification and jump to the live product page.
  3. Move it to Responded or Closed. Select several to update a batch at once.
  4. Send the member a notification when you have pricing for them.

Accounts and credits

Run the user and credit ledger

User Management

Every account with its credit position, topped by total credit exposure, active manufacturer count and pending approvals.

  • Filter the ledger, including by minimum views, likes or shares.
  • Approve or reject a business account without leaving the ledger.
  • Adjust an individual's credits, and open their full ledger history.
  • Open a user's registration documents. Those links are deliberately time-limited, so a copied link stops working — reopen from here.
  • Export the filtered ledger to CSV, or create an account manually.

Set what a credit is worth

Settings → credit controls

Three numbers govern the whole member economy.

  1. Tier 1 — Member: monthly credits for paying members.
  2. Tier 2 — Non-members: monthly credits for everyone else.
  3. Credit deduction per click: what revealing one piece costs. Commonly 5.
  4. Save. Both allowances are applied to existing accounts as well as future refreshes.

If it goes wrong“Permission denied by database policy” means the settings keys are not writable by your admin role — that is a configuration fix, not a retry.

Broadcast a notification

Settings → broadcast

  1. Choose the audience: all users, manufacturers, verified gemologists, or new onboardings.
  2. Or search by username, email or phone and target specific people.
  3. Write the subject and body.
  4. Set delivery — immediate or scheduled — and the urgency level.
  5. Send. The count of recipients is reported back, and the action is written to the admin ledger.

Catalogue and keys

Manage in-house inventory

Inventory

  • Add a piece by hand.
  • Run the bulk wizard: a CSV manifest plus the matching image files.
  • Export the current inventory to CSV.
  • Open the activity log for the full history of catalogue changes.

Issue and control API keys

AI Data Fill

Sellers cannot use AI Fill until you issue them a key. A generated key is shown once and never again — only a hash and an encrypted copy are stored.

  1. Pick the designer or manufacturer and choose how long the key should live.
  2. Generate it, then copy it out of the one-time dialog and deliver it to them.
  3. Rotate to replace a key with a fresh one and a new lifetime — do this when one is lost.
  4. Revoke to cut a key off immediately. Their fills stop working at once.

If it goes wrongA seller who has lost their key cannot be told what it was. Rotate it and send the new one.

Run fills across any catalogue

AI Data Fill → run

Unlike a seller, you are not scoped to one catalogue. Set the model and options, choose the row count, and run. The usage card tracks consumption.

Reading the platform

Dashboard

Dashboard

The opening view: platform-wide counts, the live curation feed, the appraisal queue, market pulse and the metal breakdown. Switch the metal chart between views, and export the overview as a report.

Analytics

Analytics

Daily trends over the last 30 days, engagement trends, behaviour insights and credit consumption, plus geographic engagement and catalogue-gap signals — where trending demand is outrunning stock.

Teams

Teams

Artisan workload, capacity and onboarding across your creator base, broken out by 3D designers, sketch artists and manufacturers.

Refresh and search

Console top bar

Search filters the Dashboard, Moderation and User Management views. The refresh button reloads only the section you are in, so a slow queue does not force a full reload.

SUPPORT

The reports you will actually get

Twelve tickets that account for most of the queue, with the cause and the fix. The stripe marks how urgent the report is: red needs escalation, amber needs action, green is working as designed and just needs explaining.

“My product was approved but it isn't in the catalogue”Designer · Manufacturer

CauseApproval does two things: it marks the submission approved and it publishes the piece into the live catalogue. If the second step fails, the seller sees “approved” while members see nothing.

FixAdmin: confirm the piece is genuinely missing from the live catalogue, then re-run the approval from Moderation → Archive and watch for an error. Escalate if it fails again — this is a real defect, not a stale cache.

“My uploaded pieces never show in Get it”Designer · Manufacturer

CauseReady-stock pieces are identified by a prefix on the Metal Type value. Strip or rewrite that prefix in a CSV and the piece silently drops out of the Get it catalogue while staying in the general feed.

FixExport the affected rows through Edit in Sheets, restore the Metal Type exactly as the sample CSV shows it, and sync back. Never hand-edit that column to “tidy it up”.

“I'm still stuck on the pending approval screen”Designer · Manufacturer

CauseThe account is created but not yet approved. Review is 2–3 business days; past that, it is usually sitting unworked in the queue.

FixAdmin: find them in Moderation → Verification Requests or the User Management ledger and approve or reject. They enter the business app on their next sign-in.

“This applicant has no documents attached”Admin

CauseDocuments uploaded during sign-up are held separately until the applicant confirms their email — which is precisely the state an applicant sitting in pending approval is usually in.

FixCheck the same user in User Management, which also reads the pre-confirmation holding area. Only reject for missing documents if both places are empty.

“Bulk upload said fewer rows went through than I sent”Designer · Manufacturer

CauseTwo rules skip rows: a missing required field — including having neither Product Type nor Jewelry Type — and images that failed to upload. A piece with no usable image is skipped rather than published blank.

FixThe result names each skipped row and why. Fix those rows, check the image filenames match the SKU, and re-upload just them.

“Edit in Sheets wiped out my data”Designer · Manufacturer

CauseSync overwrites every column present in the file, matching on SKU. A column left in the sheet but blanked is written as blank. A column removed from the sheet entirely is left untouched.

FixRe-export, restore the values, and sync again. Advise sellers to delete columns they are not editing rather than clearing their cells.

“AI Fill says no API key is assigned”Designer · Manufacturer

CauseNo key has been issued to that account. Saving a personal provider key is not the same thing — the platform key must exist first.

FixAdmin: issue one from AI Data Fill, copy it from the one-time dialog and send it to the seller. If they lost an existing key, rotate rather than re-issue.

“The document link you sent me has stopped working”Admin

CauseRegistration document links expire on purpose, so a copied link never becomes a permanent handle on someone's business papers.

FixReopen the document from User Management, which mints a fresh link. Do not paste these links into shared channels or tickets.

“My credits dropped by five, not one”Member

CauseWorking as designed. The cost of revealing one piece is a platform setting, commonly 5 credits.

FixExplain the rate and the monthly refresh. If a member needs more headroom, membership raises the allowance. Admins can adjust an individual's balance from the ledger.

“The demand map is locked”Designer

CauseGeographic analytics are a Premium feature for designers. Manufacturers always have them.

FixPoint the designer at the upgrade banner in their profile. If a manufacturer reports this, it is a real bug — escalate.

“I never got the setup questions”Member

CauseThe taste profile appears about 45 seconds after first reaching the home feed, and only for members who have not completed it. Business and admin accounts never see it.

FixAsk them to stay on the home feed for a minute. Answers can also be changed from the profile at any time.

“Sign-up says my email is already registered”Member · Designer · Manufacturer

CauseAn account exists on that address — often one created earlier and never confirmed.

FixSend them to Log in and have the confirmation mail resent, or use password reset. Creating a second account on the same address is blocked, and for business applications the check runs before any documents upload.

TERMS

Words used precisely

Terms that mean something specific here and are easy to misread in a ticket.

Credits
What a member spends to reveal a piece's full specification. Allowance refreshes monthly; the per-reveal cost is an admin setting.
Get it
The ready-to-buy catalogue — pieces available now rather than made to order. Identified by a prefix on Metal Type that must not be edited away.
Moderation status
Every submitted piece is pending, approved or rejected. Shown as a badge on the seller's product cards and in their Approvals tab.
SKU
The seller's own code for a piece. It is the match key for bulk image naming and for every Edit in Sheets sync.
Edit in Sheets
Export catalogue to CSV, edit anywhere, sync back. Present columns overwrite; absent columns are left alone.
AI Fill
Completing empty product attributes from the piece's images. Sellers are scoped to their own catalogue; admins are not.
Master key
The API key an admin issues to a seller so AI Fill will run. Displayed once at generation, then only rotatable or revocable.
Verification request
A business applicant awaiting document review. Distinct from a product submission awaiting moderation.
Studded vs plain
Whether a piece carries stones. Studded opens the stone specification fields; switching back clears them.
Demand level
A High / Medium / Rising banding on a seller's pieces, derived from member engagement.
Secret board
A member's private collection. Saving to one is invisible to everyone else.
Quote request
A member's priced-piece enquiry carrying the exact metal and stone specification. Worked by admins on the quote desk.
